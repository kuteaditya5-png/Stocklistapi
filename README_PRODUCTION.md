# StockLens AI v2.0 Stable Production

Production release created from the validated v2.0.1 build.

- Public Strategy Lab and all v0.7–v2.0 research buttons removed.
- Frozen validated monthly model preserved; ranking weights were not retuned.
- 1-Month Investing, Intraday Trading, and Accuracy & Backtest remain available.
- Research endpoints remain internal in the codebase for future controlled validation, but are not exposed in the production dashboard.
- Vercel root dashboard is synchronized through `api/dashboard.py`.

Historical results are research outputs and are not guarantees of future returns.
